# The egg minigame

Read before changing the egg, its phone behaviour or its terminal form. Moved from CLAUDE.md unchanged.

- **Egg minigame**: `components/egg/useEggGame.js` runs one rAF loop that draws into the hero `<pre>` or the terminal's inline one. Per-frame state stays in refs, never React state; only stage/result/stats are state. During a round (stages 1–4) the game logic runs every frame whether or not the pot is on screen (only the raster is skipped); at rest (stage 0 or 5) the loop idles while the pot is off screen or the tab hidden, and any scroll, resize, key, pointer, popstate, stage or mode change wakes it. Every per-frame step is scaled by elapsed time, so speed doesn't depend on the display's refresh rate. The character grid (resolution) comes from `ui.eggFontPx` via `src/lib/egg/grid.js`; smaller font = more cells in the same frame.
- **Egg on a phone** (≤600px, touch; `CONTEXT.md` "held"): the hero's egg panel is exactly the band between the bars (`100svh − --nav-h − --bar-h`: svh, not dvh, so Safari's toolbar can't resize the pot mid-round; the header keeps its 108px for the result's stats, the pot scales to what's left through `--egg-scale`). Starting a round scrolls its top to just under the nav (`parkEgg` in `App.jsx`, called from `startEgg` at stage 0, so a tap and the keyboard alike; never from the later pull / play-again presses, which are timed and must not move the page; the panel's own `scroll-margin-top: var(--nav-h)`, not a section jump's `--nav-offset`, whose 26px would come out of the band); the embedded shell's `./egg` does the same (and only `./egg`: `cd ~` still goes to the top of the hero), from a project page too (once the hero is back). The hero can still settle after the page lands (measured on a loaded machine: +40px, then back, and the browser's scroll anchoring moves the scroll with it but not back), so `keepAligned` (`src/lib/nav.js`) re-parks the panel for 1.5s whenever the hero or page changes size and on a short timer ladder, until the visitor scrolls or presses something (an event that began before the park doesn't count: the Enter that ran `./egg` is still on its way to `window` when React lands the leave inside that dispatch, and it once stopped the re-park at once: a load-dependent flake, 6 in 16 under CPU load, 0 in 16 after); a smooth park is left alone for its first 500ms so the re-park doesn't cancel it, and a new park replaces the old one. A completed tap in the egg frame **holds** it (`src/lib/egg/held.js`, applied in `useEggGame`; a mouse never does; held on `pointerup` inside, because a swipe that starts on the pot is scrolling the page and ends in a `pointercancel`, and holding on `pointerdown` left the next swipe turning the egg: a scroll trap; a touch landing outside lets go at once): the pot's `touch-action` goes `pan-y` → `pinch-zoom` (`[data-held] .pre`), so one finger turns the egg and the page doesn't scroll from the pot, while drags anywhere else still scroll (no page lock, so no trap); a thin outline shows it. A touch outside the frame, the round ending, or leaving the gui (`egg.release()`; the terminal block also releases when it unmounts) lets go; the pot scrolling off screen doesn't. Terminal mode's inline `./egg` is the same on touch (`components/shell/TermEgg.jsx`): a tap on the pot only holds it, the stages are buttons under it plus `quit` (no ctrl-c on a phone), the key-hint lines leave the status text, which wraps (its title line is ~76 characters), and the block fills the visible output area (`src/lib/egg/fit.js`: the pot scaled to what's left after the measured status (CSS reserves its tallest, 9 lines), the buttons and padding, floor 50%). It ends above the area's bottom padding so the shell's pin-to-bottom on focus lands where the block already is (a 23px jump between press and release once sent a button's click to the wrong element), and it aligns its own top to the area's top on mount, every resize, `visualViewport` change and scroll, because iOS gives the screen back to the page in steps after a keyboard closes and the shell's pin lets go mid-change. On touch a shortcut tab only plays its typing animation (it never focuses the prompt: that opened the keyboard) and starting `./egg` puts an open on-screen keyboard away (only while the frame is marked `data-kb`: an iPad with a hardware keyboard counts as touch but has none, and its Enter and ctrl-c need the prompt). The block marks itself `data-no-focus` so the shell pane's click-to-focus skips it. The phone panel has a 360px floor so a short narrow desktop window can't squash it.
